# Code of Conduct

This code applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other participation in this repository's community spaces.

## Expected behavior

Treat people with respect regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. Give specific, constructive feedback about the work. Assume good intent while taking reported harm seriously, respect differing opinions, and accept reasonable moderation decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, personal attacks, repeated intimidation, spam, and publishing another person's private information without permission are unacceptable. Do not use project spaces to target or retaliate against someone who raises a concern.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainer at [hire@abdulrdeveloper.me](mailto:hire@abdulrdeveloper.me). Include links, dates, and a short account of what happened; redact information that is unnecessary to understand the report.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Information may need to be shared with people responsible for investigating or addressing the incident; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised. Use the [security policy](SECURITY.md) for software vulnerabilities.

## Moderation

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, request a change in behavior, close or reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions should consider the context, severity, and repeated nature of the behavior. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.

If you believe a moderation decision missed relevant context, explain that context through the same private contact route.

Fork owners should update the reporting contact and identify who moderates their own community.
