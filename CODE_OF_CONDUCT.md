# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes respectful participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-related interactions.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and patient.
- Discuss the work and its impact; avoid personal attacks.
- Respect different perspectives and accept good-faith feedback.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and other people's boundaries.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Report a concern

Send conduct concerns to [hire@abdulrdeveloper.me](mailto:hire@abdulrdeveloper.me). Include the relevant links, what happened, and any immediate concerns. Avoid posting private information publicly or including unrelated personal details.

Reports should be handled with discretion, with information shared only as needed to assess and address the concern. A specific outcome or response time is not guaranteed.

## Moderation

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, request changes in behavior, or restrict participation. The response should reflect the severity and context of the behavior; threats or repeated harassment may lead to immediate restrictions.

For software vulnerabilities, use the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md). For ordinary bugs and questions, see [Support](SUPPORT.md).
